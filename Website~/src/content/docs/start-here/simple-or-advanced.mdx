The AvatarDoc inspector has two modes. <Label k="ad.ui.mode.advanced" />, the default, shows every tool. <Label k="ad.ui.mode.simple" /> shows only what most avatars need. Switching changes what you see, never what you made.

## What each mode shows [#what-each-mode-shows]

| Part | Simple | Advanced |
|---|---|---|
| Header | the status line, <Label k="ad.ui.ready.show" /> while there is a problem, <Label k="ad.ui.summary" /> and the memory bar, plus <Label k="ad.ui.settings_fold" /> once a setting in it is changed | the same, with <Label k="ad.ui.settings_fold" /> always there |
| Toolbar | <Label k="ad.ui.add.toggle" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.radial" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.submenu" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.outfit" /> and the search field | every add, also <Label k="ad.ui.add.trigger" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.override" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.preset" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.grabbable" /> (see [Grabbable](../../reference/grabbable/)), <Label k="ad.ui.add.animator" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.menu_asset" />, <Label k="ad.ui.parameters.add" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.add.contact" />, plus <Label k="ad.ui.toolbar.capture" />, <Label k="ad.ui.toolbar.convert" />, <Label k="ad.ui.wheel" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.tagfilter" /> |
| A toggle's panel | <Label k="ad.ui.name" />, <Label k="ad.ui.icon" />, <Label k="ad.ui.default_on" />, <Label k="ad.ui.in_menu" />, and the <Label k="ad.ui.objects" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> sections | <Label k="ad.ui.name" />, <Label k="ad.ui.icon" />, <Label k="ad.ui.default_on" />, <Label k="ad.ui.in_menu" /> and the <Label k="ad.ui.objects" /> section, plus <Label k="ad.ui.style" />, <Label k="ad.ui.style.raw_label" />, the Builds line, <Label k="ad.ui.group" />, <Label k="ad.ui.preview" /> and the <Label k="ad.ui.advanced_fold" /> fold. <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> and the other sections show once you add them with <Label k="ad.ui.add_effect" />. |
| <Label k="ad.ui.add_effect" /> on a toggle | <Label k="ad.ui.objects" />, <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" />, <Label k="ad.ui.targets.properties" /> | the same, plus <Label k="ad.ui.materials" />, <Label k="ad.ui.targets.animations" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.targets.follows" /> |
| A radial's panel | <Label k="ad.ui.name" />, <Label k="ad.ui.icon" />, <Label k="ad.ui.default_value" />, <Label k="ad.ui.in_menu" />, and the <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> section | the same, plus <Label k="ad.ui.style" />, <Label k="ad.ui.style.raw_label" />, the Builds line, <Label k="ad.ui.preview" /> and the <Label k="ad.ui.advanced_fold" /> fold |
| <Label k="ad.ui.add_effect" /> on a radial | <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" />, <Label k="ad.ui.targets.properties" /> | the same, plus <Label k="ad.ui.targets.animations" /> |
| A submenu's panel | <Label k="ad.ui.name" />, <Label k="ad.ui.icon" />, and how many of its slots are used | the same, plus <Label k="ad.ui.style" />, <Label k="ad.ui.style.raw_label" />, the Builds line and the <Label k="ad.ui.advanced_fold" /> fold |
| The welcome card | <Label k="ad.ui.welcome.toggle" />, <Label k="ad.ui.welcome.outfit" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.welcome.quickstart" /> | the same, plus a Convert start when the avatar has something AvatarDoc can convert |
| Any other entry | its whole panel, with a note when Simple cannot add more like it | its whole panel |

Right click menus are the same in both modes.

## Switch modes [#switch-modes]

Press <Label k="ad.ui.mode.simple" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.mode.advanced" /> at the top of the AvatarDoc inspector. The AvatarDoc window, under **Tools > DocMe > AvatarDoc**, has the same switch.

The choice belongs to you, not to one component:

- Every AvatarDoc component follows it, in the inspector and in the window.
- Unity remembers it the next time you open the project.
- Switching in one place switches every open AvatarDoc inspector and window at once.

## Nothing is lost [#nothing-is-lost]

Switching only changes what the inspector shows. It writes nothing to your component and builds nothing again.

Simple never hides what you made. The tree shows every entry, and a part that holds something keeps showing. For example, a toggle that uses <Label k="ad.ui.materials" /> still shows its <Label k="ad.ui.materials" /> section in Simple. A tag filter you set in Advanced pauses in Simple, and works again when you switch back.
