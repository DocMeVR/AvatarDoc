In the picture, 1 is the <Label k="ad.ui.touch.anchor" />, the bone the touch area follows. 2 is <Label k="ad.ui.touch.tags" />, the body parts that count as a touch. 3 is the blend shape the trigger fades in.

At the end of this page your avatar blushes when a hand comes close to the top of its head.

## Before you start [#before-you-start]

- An AvatarDoc component on your avatar, as in [Your first toggle](../first-toggle/).
- The inspector in <Label k="ad.ui.mode.advanced" /> mode, the default. Simple mode cannot add triggers: [Simple or Advanced?](../simple-or-advanced/) explains the modes.
- The avatar's Animator set to Humanoid, with an Avatar assigned, so AvatarDoc can find the Head bone.
- A blush blend shape on the avatar's face. This page uses one named Blush.

## Steps [#steps]

<Steps>

1. In the Hierarchy, select the object that holds your AvatarDoc component.

2. In the inspector's toolbar, press <Label k="ad.ui.more" />, then <Label k="ad.ui.add.contact" />. On a wide inspector, it sits on the toolbar itself.

   You should now see <Label k="ad.ui.add.contact_starts.headpat" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.contact_starts.eye_touch" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.add.contact_starts.nose_boop" />.

3. Pick <Label k="ad.ui.add.contact_starts.headpat" />.

   You should now see a trigger named Headpat, selected under <Label k="ad.ui.triggers" /> in the tree. In its panel, <Label k="ad.ui.touch.mode" /> is <Label k="ad.ui.touch.follow" />, so the effect grows as a hand comes closer. The <Label k="ad.ui.touch.anchor" /> is the Head bone, and <Label k="ad.ui.touch.tags" /> has **Hand** and **Finger** ticked.

4. In the panel's <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> section, press the shape button of the row. It shows the name of your face mesh.

   You should now see a list of blend shapes, with a search field.

5. Type `Blush` and pick your blush shape.

   You should now see the row show the blush shape, with <Label k="ad.ui.weight" /> at 100.

6. In the Scene view, look at the top of the avatar's head.

   You should now see the trigger's sphere there, with handles to move it and to change its size.

</Steps>

## Test it [#test-it]

With Gesture Manager:

1. Enter Play mode with Gesture Manager, as in [Your first toggle](../first-toggle/#test-it).
2. In the GestureManager inspector, open the **Tools** tab.
3. Unfold **Clickable Contacts** and tick **Enabled**.
4. In the Game view, click and hold on top of the avatar's head.

The avatar blushes while you hold. If the Game view does not show the avatar, unfold **Scene Camera** in the same tab and press its button: the Game view then follows the Scene view.

You can also upload the avatar and pat its head in VRChat. <Label k="ad.ui.touch.self" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.touch.others" /> are both on, so your own hands count as well as other players' hands.
