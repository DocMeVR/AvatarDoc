Each problem here is named by what you see. Read the cause, then follow the fix. If a warning or an error shows in the inspector or in NDMF's console, look it up in [Messages](../../reference/messages/) first: every message has its own entry with what to do.

## My blinking or lip sync stops while a toggle is on [#blinking-stops]

### Likely cause

The toggle holds a shape that VRChat uses for blinking or for lip sync. While the toggle is on, AvatarDoc pauses VRChat's blinking or lip sync so that the two do not fight. They come back when the toggle is off. This happens with a Hold the rest rule that has <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_blink" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_visemes" /> turned on, with a <Label k="ad.ui.seg.pin" /> row on an eyelid or mouth shape, and with a trigger whose effect moves such a shape.

### Fix

1. In the tree, select the toggle or the trigger.
2. In its <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> section, find the rule line under the rows. Turn off <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_blink" /> to keep blinking, and <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_visemes" /> to keep lip sync.
3. If a row sets an eyelid or a mouth shape on purpose, the pause is the expected result. To keep blinking, remove that row.
4. If blinking still stops after an upload, look for a warning that says blinking or lip sync is left to another system. A sleep or AFK layer of your avatar then owns it. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-follow-distance-tracking-left-to).

How the pause works: [Blinking, lip sync and face tracking](../../how-it-works/blinking-and-lip-sync/). The controls: [Hold the rest of a face](../../guides/hold-the-rest/).

## A toggle never turns off, or something stays changed after I turn it off [#toggle-never-turns-off]

### Likely cause

Something else writes the same object, shape or material after the toggle does. These are the usual ones:

- Two controls change the same thing. The one built later wins: later menu entries first, and triggers after every menu control. An override wins over every control.
- A preset, or a contact in <Label k="ad.ui.touch.flip" /> mode, changes the toggle's own parameter and turns it on again.
- A blend shape row is set to <Label k="ad.ui.seg.pin" />. While the toggle is off, a Pin row writes nothing. When the toggle turns off, and when the avatar loads, it sets the shape back to its value in the scene once, unless another layer owns the shape, and from then on whatever else moves the shape decides it.

### Fix

1. Select the toggle. If its row has a <Label k="ad.ui.chip.problem" /> chip, hold the pointer over it. A note that says the target is changed by more than one control names the other controls. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-info-target-shared).
2. Give each control its own targets, or put toggles that exclude each other in one exclusive group, as in [Make one-of-many choices](../../guides/one-of-many/).
3. For a blend shape that should return to its scene value, set the row to <Label k="ad.ui.seg.fixed" />. A Fixed row writes the scene value while the toggle is off.
4. Check the presets and the contacts in <Label k="ad.ui.touch.flip" /> mode: one of them may write the toggle's parameter. See [One button for several settings](../../guides/presets/) and [React to touch](../../guides/react-to-touch/).
5. Test in Play mode with Gesture Manager, and switch the toggle off. See [Your first toggle](../../start-here/first-toggle/#test-it).

### Still stuck

Press <Label k="ad.ui.summary" /> in the header and keep the text for [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## My menu, or a toggle, is missing in VRChat [#menu-missing]

### Likely cause

AvatarDoc built the avatar without that control, or put it where you did not look. These are the usual causes:

- The AvatarDoc component is switched off. AvatarDoc skips a component that is switched off, and the tree shows its rows with a <Label k="ad.ui.disabled_chip" /> chip.
- The toggle has <Label k="ad.ui.in_menu" /> turned off. It has no button then, and the row has a <Label k="ad.ui.chip.no_menu" /> chip.
- The entry is built for the other platform only. Its row shows a <Label k="ad.ui.chip.pc_only" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.chip.quest_only" /> chip and is dimmed.
- The component installs into a submenu with <Label k="ad.ui.install" />, so the button is inside that submenu and not on the first page.
- A page held more than eight controls, so the rest went onto a **More** page.
- An error stopped the build. An error stops the upload, so the avatar you see in VRChat is an older one.

### Fix

1. Select the object that holds the component, and check that its checkbox in the inspector is ticked.
2. Look at the tree: it shows the menu as it will be built, with the submenus and the **More** pages. Find your control there.
3. Open <Label k="ad.ui.settings_fold" /> and check <Label k="ad.ui.install" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.platform" />.
4. Select the toggle and check <Label k="ad.ui.in_menu" /> and its <Label k="ad.ui.platform" />.
5. Upload again, and read NDMF's console for errors. [Messages](../../reference/messages/) explains each one.

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## The upload stops: the synced parameters need more bits than VRChat allows [#too-many-bits]

### Likely cause

VRChat gives an avatar 256 bits for synced parameters, and the avatar is over. A synced toggle costs 1 bit. A synced exclusive group or a synced radial costs 8. With VRCFury installed, the same check is only a warning, because VRCFury makes the parameters smaller at upload.

### Fix

1. Read the memory bar in the inspector's header. It shows the avatar's own bits, the bits AvatarDoc adds and the bits that are free.
2. Turn off <Label k="ad.ui.synced" /> on toggles that other players do not need to see. They then cost nothing.
3. Put toggles of one kind into an exclusive group. A group costs 8 bits however many toggles it holds.
4. Take the menu items of the avatar that you never use out of the build with <Label k="ad.ui.existing.remove" />. That frees their parameters.

[Fit in VRChat's parameter memory](../../guides/parameter-memory/) goes through these in order. How the count works: [Parameters, sync and memory](../../how-it-works/parameters-and-memory/).

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## My menu has a "More" page that I did not make [#more-page]

### Likely cause

VRChat shows at most eight controls on one page. When a submenu holds more, AvatarDoc keeps the first seven where they are and moves the rest onto a **More** page that takes the eighth place. The tree shows these pages as **More (page 2)**, **More (page 3)** and so on.

### Fix

1. Select the submenu. A row with **8 of 8** is full.
2. Move some of its controls into a submenu of their own: drag them in the tree.
3. Check the tree again. The **More** page goes away when everything fits.

See [Organise the menu](../../guides/organise-the-menu/) and [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-info-menu-split).

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A toggle works on PC, but it is missing or wrong on Quest [#quest]

### Likely cause

AvatarDoc builds for the platform that the editor targets. An entry that is set for PC only is not built for the Quest copy of the avatar. Or the two copies have different parameter lists. VRChat syncs parameters by their place in the list, so a list that differs shows the other platform's players the wrong toggles.

### Fix

1. Check the build target of the project: Android and iOS count as Quest, everything else as PC. The line under <Label k="ad.ui.platform" /> in <Label k="ad.ui.settings_fold" /> names the platform the editor builds for.
2. Select the toggle and open its <Label k="ad.ui.advanced_fold" /> fold. Check <Label k="ad.ui.platform" />.
3. Do not delete an entry from one copy of the avatar. Set its <Label k="ad.ui.platform" /> instead, because then it keeps its parameter in the same place.
4. If a warning says the parameters differ at a position, rebuild both copies from the same menu order. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-platform-parity).

[Different setups for PC and Quest](../../guides/pc-and-quest/) shows the settings.

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A toggle I converted behaves differently from the original [#converted-toggle-differs]

### Likely cause

AvatarDoc does not carry over everything from VRCFury or Modular Avatar. The Convert window lists a note for each part that was left behind. These are the usual differences:

- A transition animation, a security lock, a separate state that only you see, a hold button and an inverted rest logic are not carried over.
- An AvatarDoc toggle switches at once, or fades with <Label k="ad.ui.blend" />.
- The entry gets a parameter of its own. An outside tool that set the old parameter, such as an OSC app, must set the new one.
- A hand made layer that rested somewhere else is not converted at all: the window lists it with its reason.

### Fix

1. If you have not changed anything else yet, use **Edit > Undo**. Convert undoes in one step.
2. Open the Convert window again and read the notes of the row. [What each note means](../../reference/convert-window/#what-each-note-means) explains every one.
3. After you convert, select the new entry and check its <Label k="ad.ui.target.off_value" /> fields, <Label k="ad.ui.blend" /> and parameter name against the old toggle.
4. To keep the old behaviour, leave that item as it is, with <Label k="ad.ui.import.keep" /> in the Convert window.

See [Convert from VRCFury or Modular Avatar](../../guides/convert/).

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A piece of clothing keeps the wrong body shape, or two pieces fight over a shape [#shapes-conflict]

### Likely cause

Two AvatarDoc Shapes pieces hold the same shape, and different layers switch them: two toggles, or a toggle and a trigger or a merged animator. The later layer decides the shape. While the later piece is on, its value holds. While it is off, the shape goes back to rest, even when the other piece is on. A piece that nothing switches loses to one that is switched.

### Fix

1. Read the warning. It names both pieces and the shape. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-shapes-conflict).
2. Put both pieces on one toggle, or put their toggles in one exclusive group. Then one animation switches both, or only one piece is on at a time.
3. If a note says nothing turns the object on, add a toggle or an outfit set for it, or take the component off the piece.

[AvatarDoc Shapes](../../reference/avatardoc-shapes/#when-two-pieces-hold-one-shape) lists what decides the winner.

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## Hold the rest does not hold a shape [#hold-the-rest-not-holding]

### Likely cause

A rule holds only the shapes that something else moves. It leaves these shapes to something else:

- the shapes the entry has rows for, and the shapes a toggle's own animations move
- the shape a blend shape trigger follows, and the shapes an AvatarDoc Shapes component drives
- the blink and mouth shapes, unless <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_blink" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_visemes" /> is on
- a shape that only something outside your avatar moves, such as a dance in an MMD world

### Fix

1. Select the toggle and open the rule line under its rows. Its note says how many shapes it can hold, up to.
2. To hold the eyes or the mouth too, turn on <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_blink" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold_visemes" />.
3. For an MMD world, use the world's own settings. AvatarDoc holds only what the avatar's own animations move.
4. If an error says only a toggle or a trigger with effects can hold the rest, move the rule to one of those. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-error-shape-set-not-a-toggle).

See [Hold the rest of a face](../../guides/hold-the-rest/).

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A touch or PhysBone trigger never fires [#touch-never-fires]

### Likely cause

For a contact, an error at upload names the cause:

- no object is set for <Label k="ad.ui.touch.anchor" />
- no tag is ticked under <Label k="ad.ui.touch.tags" />
- both <Label k="ad.ui.touch.self" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.touch.others" /> are off

For a PhysBone trigger, no PhysBone is picked. A trigger that flips a toggle does nothing on a platform where that toggle is not built.

### Fix

1. Select the trigger and read the messages on its panel.
2. Set <Label k="ad.ui.touch.anchor" />, tick at least one tag under <Label k="ad.ui.touch.tags" />, and keep <Label k="ad.ui.touch.self" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.touch.others" /> on. The default is **Finger** and **Hand**, and both switches on.
3. For a PhysBone trigger, pick the PhysBone in <Label k="ad.ui.touch.physbone" />.
4. Test the touch in Play mode with Gesture Manager's **Clickable Contacts**, or in VRChat. The scene preview cannot sense a real touch.

See [React to touch](../../guides/react-to-touch/) and [React to PhysBone grabs, poses and stretches](../../guides/react-to-physbones/).

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A trigger with conditions never fires [#condition-trigger-never-fires]

### Likely cause

The trigger has no condition, or a group with nothing in it, or it reads a parameter that the avatar does not declare. A parameter that nothing declares is treated as a Float, so a condition made for a whole number or for true or false may not work.

### Fix

1. Select the trigger. Under <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.when" />, check that at least one condition is set.
2. Remove a group that is empty, or add a condition to it with <Label k="ad.ui.when.add_and" />.
3. If a warning says nothing declares the parameter, add it under **Parameters** with its real type. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-parameter-type-unknown).
4. Check <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.gate" />: the trigger fires only while that toggle is in the state you chose.

See [Link effects to another toggle or a parameter](../../guides/link-to-a-toggle-or-parameter/).

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A gesture face does not show with face tracking [#gesture-face-tracking]

### Likely cause

- The face is set to <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.paused" /> in the <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.title" /> window. While face tracking is on, a paused face changes nothing on purpose: face tracking has the face.
- <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.tracking" /> names a parameter your face tracking setup does not set, or one it sets the other way round, so AvatarDoc thinks face tracking is on all the time.
- The parameter is the Lip Tracking toggle of the VRCFT face tracking templates, LipTrackingActive, which is saved: left on while you play without face tracking, it keeps every face set to <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.paused" /> off.
- Other players see a face set to <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.paused" /> while you do not: the parameter is not synced, so to them face tracking is never on. The window says so under the field.
- Face tracking moves different shapes than the face does, so both show at once and the face looks wrong rather than missing.
- The face does show, but face tracking takes the shapes back the moment you let go of the gesture. That is how a face set to <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.wins" /> ends.

### Fix

1. Select the object with the AvatarDoc component and press <Label k="ad.ui.add.gesture_faces" /> in the toolbar.
2. In the gesture's row, set <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.row.tracking" /> to <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.wins" /> if the face should show over face tracking while you hold the gesture.
3. Check <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.tracking" />: it must name the Bool parameter that is on while face tracking is on, such as LipTrackingActive, synced if your setup has one. Pick it from the list beside the field, type its name, or empty the field for <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.tracking.none" /> when the avatar has no face tracking.
4. Playing without face tracking: switch the Lip Tracking toggle of the face tracking menu off in game, so the faces set to <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.paused" /> show.
5. To keep face tracking from moving the face's other shapes while a face shows, give the face's trigger a Hold the rest rule: select the trigger in the tree and press <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.hold" /> in its <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> section. See [Hold the rest of a face](../../guides/hold-the-rest/).
6. Press <Label k="ad.ui.gestures.make" />, upload, and test with face tracking on.

More about the choices: [Gesture faces window](../../reference/gesture-faces-window/#while-tracked).

### Still stuck

Press <Label k="ad.ui.summary" /> in the header and keep the text for [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A blend shape trigger does nothing [#blend-shape-trigger-does-nothing]

### Likely cause

The trigger follows a shape, and nothing in the avatar's FX animator moves that shape. Or VRChat moves the shape itself, as it does for blinking and lip sync, outside every animation, so the trigger never sees it move. Or the shape moves in a Gesture or Action animation, outside the FX layer. Or the trigger has requirements in several cards: it reads only the card its shape is in.

### Fix

1. Read the warning on the trigger. It names the shape. See [the message about no FX animation](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-link-no-fx-clip) and [the one about tracked shapes](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-link-source-tracked).
2. Pick a shape that one of your own FX animations moves.
3. If the shape moves in an animation outside FX, move that animation into the FX layer. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-link-clips-outside-fx).
4. If a warning says the trigger reads only one card, move the conditions you need into that card. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-requirements-one-card).

See [Drive effects from a blend shape](../../guides/follow-a-blend-shape/).

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A toggle is in the menu, but it does nothing [#toggle-does-nothing]

### Likely cause

The toggle has no effects, or its targets are gone. A custom parameter name with no effects only drives the parameter, so something else has to read it.

### Fix

1. Select the toggle and read its messages. A warning that it has no targets means nothing was built for it. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-no-effects).
2. Press <Label k="ad.ui.add_effect" /> and add an object, a blend shape, a material or a property.
3. If a row points at something that is gone, or at an object outside the avatar, open the row and fix or remove it. See [a missing target](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-target-missing) and [a target outside the avatar](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-target-outside-avatar).
4. If the toggle drives a custom parameter on purpose, check that a merged animator, VRCFury or Modular Avatar reads it. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-parameter-control-unused).

See [Toggle clothing and accessories](../../guides/toggle-clothing/).

## A colour or glow does not change in VRChat [#property-not-in-game]

### Likely cause

The material's shader locks the material at upload and fixed the property at its value. Poiyomi does this with every property that is not marked as animated. A material that was already locked before the property was marked keeps the old values too. Poiyomi leaves the materials locked after an upload, so a material locked at an earlier upload does not change in Play mode either.

A material swapped in outside AvatarDoc's own material rows, by your own animations, an AvatarDoc custom animation, a Modular Avatar Material Swap or VRCFury, is not checked by AvatarDoc, so no warning names it.

### Fix

1. Select the toggle, radial or trigger and read its messages. A warning that a Poiyomi material does not mark the property as animated names the materials. Press <Label k="ad.ui.poiyomi.mark_animated" /> under it. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-poiyomi-not-animated).
2. A warning that a Poiyomi material is locked with the property fixed has no button. Select the material, press **Unlock Shader** at the top of its inspector, right-click the property and choose **Animated (when locked)**. Then lock it again, or leave it: Poiyomi locks it at upload. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-poiyomi-baked).
3. For a material swapped in outside AvatarDoc's material rows, and for another shader that locks its materials, mark the property as animated in the material's own inspector, then lock the material again.
4. When you add a property with the [Add to AvatarDoc window](../../reference/add-window/#poiyomi), leave <Label k="ad.ui.add_from.mark" /> ticked: it marks the property as you add it.
5. Upload again and test in VRChat. Play mode shows the change only while the material is unlocked: a material locked at an earlier upload behaves there as it does in VRChat.

More about the marks: [On a Poiyomi material](../../guides/colour-and-glow/#poiyomi).

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## Add to AvatarDoc is not in the right-click menu [#add-item-missing]

### Likely cause

- You right-clicked a material property. Unity offers that right click only to its Animation window, so it never shows the AvatarDoc item, with any shader.
- The blend shape was never set since the mesh was imported. Unity draws such a slider without the property the item needs.
- The item is there but greyed out: the mesh is not part of an avatar in the scene. The material's header item is also greyed out when no selected mesh under an avatar uses the material, such as a material selected in the Project window.

### Fix

1. For a material property, select the mesh in the Hierarchy, press the three dots at the right end of the material's header under it, and choose **Add to AvatarDoc...**. It works with every shader, Poiyomi included. Pick the property in the window.
2. Or use the mesh's component menu: press the three dots at the right end of the Skinned Mesh Renderer or Mesh Renderer header, or right-click the header, and choose **Add to AvatarDoc...**. Pick the shape, or the material and the property, in the window.
3. For a blend shape, move its slider once, then right-click it again.
4. For a greyed out item, move the mesh under the avatar, the object that holds the VRChat Avatar Descriptor, and select the mesh, not the material in the Project window.

More about the window: [Add to AvatarDoc window](../../reference/add-window/).

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## Some objects were not found when copying a menu [#copy-not-found]

### Likely cause

- The object has another name, or sits in another place, on the version you copy to: the base avatar renamed or moved it in its new release, or you did on one version only.
- The object is not on that version at all, such as an accessory you added to one version only.
- The object is there, but not with the component the entry needs, such as a mesh renderer or a second PhysBone.
- Several objects of one name sit side by side, and the two versions do not have the same number of them. The window then matches them by their order and says so in a note, and the match can be the wrong one.
- The object is marked Pick by hand when copying: the window always lists it for you to pick, even when the other version has an object at the same place.

### Fix

1. In the <Label k="ad.ui.copy_to.title" /> window, under <Label k="ad.ui.copy_to.objects" />, pick the object to use for each row in <Label k="ad.ui.copy_to.use" />. When the field already holds an object, it is a suggestion: the object at the same place, or the only object of that name. Check that it is the right one. The field takes only an object of the version you copy to.
2. A row that says **Pick one before copying** needs an object before <Label k="ad.ui.copy_to.run" /> works: an empty path root would make the clips move other objects, and an empty Hold the rest mesh, grabbable object, contact anchor or PhysBone is an error that stops AvatarDoc building on that avatar.
3. Leave any other field empty to fix it later: the copy leaves that row of the entry empty, the build skips the row, and the entry's messages name it. See [A target is missing](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-target-missing).
4. For a note about objects of the same name, rename them on both versions so each name is used once, or check the copied rows after copying.
5. For an object you want to choose on every version, such as one a world drop holds, mark it on the version you copy from: right-click its row in the entry and choose <Label k="ad.ui.copy_to.by_hand" />.

More about how the window finds objects: [Copy to another avatar window](../../reference/copy-window/#how-objects-are-found).

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## VRChat or VRCFury warns about mixed Write Defaults [#write-defaults-warning]

### Likely cause

The layers of the avatar do not all use the same Write Defaults setting. AvatarDoc then builds its ordinary layers with Write Defaults on, and a warning names the layers on each side. Separately, AvatarDoc's pin layers are meant to have Write Defaults off, so VRCFury may report them as a mix.

### Fix

1. To remove AvatarDoc's own warning, make the avatar's layers agree. The first line of <Label k="ad.ui.settings_fold" /> shows the setting AvatarDoc uses.
2. When VRCFury offers to fix a mix and AvatarDoc has <Label k="ad.ui.seg.pin" /> rows or <Label k="ad.ui.touch.follow" /> triggers, choose **Skip**. If you press **Auto-Fix**, AvatarDoc turns its pin layers back off at the end of the upload.

See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-write-defaults-mixed) and [Layers, Write Defaults and pinned layers](../../how-it-works/layers-and-pins/).

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## The upload says a menu icon is too large [#icon-too-large]

### Likely cause

VRChat's upload check rejects an icon that is larger than 256 pixels on a side while its import size allows more, or that is not compressed.

### Fix

1. Select the texture in the Project window.
2. In its import settings, set **Max Size** to 256, and set **Compression** to anything but None. Press Apply.
3. Or let AvatarDoc make the icon: <Label k="ad.ui.capture" /> draws it at 256 by 256 pixels, which passes the check.

See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-error-menu-icon-too-large) and [Icon capture](../../reference/icon-capture/).

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A trigger has too many conditions [#trigger-too-complex]

### Likely cause

A trigger may have at most 4 groups of at most 4 conditions each, and at most 64 ways to turn off again. That last count is the product of the group sizes, so 4 groups of 4 conditions come to 256 and are refused. [Limits](../../reference/limits/#how-complex-a-trigger-may-be) explains the count with a table of what builds and what is refused. Beyond these caps, VRChat cannot build all the ways to turn the trigger off again, so the build stops with an error.

### Fix

1. Select the trigger and count its groups under <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.when" />.
2. Remove conditions you do not need, or merge groups.
3. If you need more, split the trigger into two triggers with the same effects.

See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-error-trigger-too-complex) and [Triggers](../../reference/triggers/#rows).

### Still stuck

See [Report a bug](../report-a-bug/).

## A toolbar button I need is not there [#toolbar-more]

### Likely cause

The inspector is narrow. The toolbar shows as many add buttons as fit, and puts the others under <Label k="ad.ui.more" />. At 360 pixels wide, it shows <Label k="ad.ui.add.toggle" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.radial" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.add.submenu" />. In Simple mode, the toolbar also leaves out the other adds on purpose.

### Fix

1. Press <Label k="ad.ui.more" /> and pick the add from its list. Each add has a one line description.
2. Or make the inspector wider, until every add shows as a button.
3. For <Label k="ad.ui.add.override" />, <Label k="ad.ui.add.preset" /> and the other adds of Advanced mode, switch the mode row at the top of the inspector to <Label k="ad.ui.mode.advanced" />.

## The tags on the tree rows, such as VRCFury or Existing, are missing [#tree-chips-hidden]

### Likely cause

The tree is narrower than 360 pixels. A narrow tree leaves out the group, condition, source, asset and existing chips, and shows a platform chip only while the entry is not built for the current platform. The rows of other tools and of the avatar's own menu stay dimmed at every width.

### Fix

1. Make the inspector wider, or use the AvatarDoc window from **Tools > DocMe > AvatarDoc**, which can be wider than the inspector.
2. The dimmed rows and the square icons still tell you that a row comes from another tool or from the avatar's own menu. The panel of the row names the tool.

See [The inspector at a glance](../../reference/inspector/#tree).
