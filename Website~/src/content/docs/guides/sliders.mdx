## When to use this

Use a radial when you want more than on and off: a smile that grows as you turn the dial, or a glow that gets brighter. VRChat shows a radial as a dial in the menu.

If you only need on and off, a toggle is simpler and costs less memory: see [Toggle clothing and accessories](../toggle-clothing/). To change a colour with a toggle, see [Change a colour or glow](../colour-and-glow/).

## Steps

<Steps>

1. In the Hierarchy, select the object that holds your AvatarDoc component.

2. In the inspector's toolbar, press <Label k="ad.ui.add.radial" />.

   You should now see a radial named New Radial, selected in the tree. Its panel has an empty <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> section.

3. In the radial's panel, type a name into <Label k="ad.ui.name" />, such as Smile, and press Enter.

4. In the <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> section, press <Label k="ad.ui.add" />.

   You should now see a list of your avatar's blend shapes, with a search field.

5. Type part of the shape's name and pick the shape.

   You should now see a row with the shape, <Label k="ad.ui.min" /> at 0 and <Label k="ad.ui.max" /> at 100.

6. On the row, set <Label k="ad.ui.min" /> to the weight you want at the lowest point of the dial, and <Label k="ad.ui.max" /> to the weight at the highest point.

7. In the panel, drag the <Label k="ad.ui.default_value" /> slider to where the dial should sit when the avatar loads. 0 is the lowest point and 1 the highest.

   <Shot name="guides/sliders-radial" alt="A Smile radial's panel with 1 the Default slider, 2 the Min and Max of its blend shape row, and 3 the Scene preview slider." />

   In the picture, 1 is the <Label k="ad.ui.default_value" /> slider, 2 the <Label k="ad.ui.min" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.max" /> of the row, and 3 the <Label k="ad.ui.preview" /> slider.

</Steps>

To drive several shapes from the same dial, add a row for each. They all move together.

To start from a face you already set up in the scene, press <Label k="ad.ui.shape_set.capture" /> in the <Label k="ad.ui.blendshapes" /> section and pick the mesh. Every shape of that mesh higher than 0 becomes a row, with its scene weight as <Label k="ad.ui.max" /> and 0 as <Label k="ad.ui.min" />.

### Slide a material value

A radial can also move a shader value, such as an emission strength or a hue shift.

<Steps>

1. In the radial's panel, press <Label k="ad.ui.add_effect" />, then <Label k="ad.ui.targets.properties" />.

   You should now see a <Label k="ad.ui.targets.properties" /> section with an empty row.

2. On the row, drag the mesh from the Hierarchy onto the renderer field, or pick it with the small circle.

3. Press <Label k="ad.ui.property.pick" /> and pick the property from the list.

   <Label k="ad.ui.max" /> now holds the material's current value.

4. Set <Label k="ad.ui.min" /> to the value at the lowest point of the dial and <Label k="ad.ui.max" /> to the value at the highest point.

</Steps>

[Change a colour or glow](../colour-and-glow/) explains the property list in more detail.

## Check it worked

- In the radial's panel, drag the <Label k="ad.ui.preview" /> slider (<Label k="ad.ui.mode.advanced" /> mode). The Scene view follows it. Press <Label k="ad.ui.preview.default" /> beside the slider to stop the preview. The preview needs NDMF's previews: turn them on under **Tools > NDM Framework > Enable Previews**.
- In Play mode with Gesture Manager, open the radial in the menu and turn the dial, as in [Your first toggle](../../start-here/first-toggle/#test-it).

## If it does not work

- The shape does not move in the game: another animation on the avatar, such as a gesture or face tracking, also moves that shape and wins over the radial. Pick a shape nothing else moves.
- The parameter memory is full: a radial costs 8 synced bits, a toggle 1. The memory bar in the inspector's header shows how many are left.
- Turning the dial changes nothing: the row's <Label k="ad.ui.min" /> and <Label k="ad.ui.max" /> hold the same value.
