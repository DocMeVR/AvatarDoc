## When to use this

Use a trigger when something should follow a state the avatar already has: hide the hair strands while the Hat toggle is on, blush while the right hand shows a thumbs up, or change the face only while sitting. A trigger has no button in the menu and costs no synced bits.

For a touch, see [React to touch](../react-to-touch/). For a PhysBone, see [React to PhysBone grabs, poses and stretches](../react-to-physbones/). For a shape that moves smoothly, see [Drive effects from a blend shape](../follow-a-blend-shape/).

This needs <Label k="ad.ui.mode.advanced" /> mode, the default.

## Steps

### Follow a toggle

<Steps>

1. In the Hierarchy, select the object that holds your AvatarDoc component.

2. In the inspector's toolbar, press <Label k="ad.ui.add.trigger" />. On a narrow inspector, it sits under <Label k="ad.ui.more" />.

   You should now see a trigger named New Trigger, selected under <Label k="ad.ui.triggers" /> in the tree.

3. In the trigger's panel, type a name into <Label k="ad.ui.name" />, such as Hat hair, and press Enter.

   You should now see <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.when" /> set to <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.source.toggle" />, the start of every new trigger.

4. In <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.toggle" />, pick the toggle to follow, such as Hat.

   The list shows every toggle of every AvatarDoc component on the avatar. A toggle of another component shows that component's object name in brackets.

5. In the list under it, choose <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.toggle.on" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.toggle.off" />.

   You should now see the <Label k="ad.ui.fires" /> line spell out the rule, such as When "Hat" is on.

6. Add the effects: drag objects onto <Label k="ad.ui.objects" />, or press <Label k="ad.ui.add_effect" /> for blend shapes, materials and properties. For the hair strands, set their rows to <Label k="ad.ui.seg.hidden" />.

</Steps>

### Follow a parameter

A parameter is a value the avatar keeps, such as the gesture of each hand. VRChat sets many of them, and other tools add their own.

<Steps>

1. In the trigger's panel, set <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.when" /> to <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.source.parameter" />.

2. Press the arrow button at the right end of <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.parameter" />.

   You should now see a list of parameters, grouped by where they come from, with a search field.

3. Type part of the name, such as `Gesture`, and pick the parameter, such as GestureRight.

   <Shot name="guides/link-to-a-toggle-or-parameter-picker" alt="A trigger with 1 When set to A parameter and 2 the Parameter field, and the parameter list open with Gesture typed in its search field." />

   In the picture, 1 is <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.when" /> and 2 the <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.parameter" /> field. The list under the arrow button shows the parameters that match the search, each with its type.

4. In <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition" />, choose the test. For a parameter that is on or off, pick <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition.is_true" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition.is_false" />. For a number, pick <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition.equals" />, <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition.not_equals" />, <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition.greater" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition.less" />, and type the number into <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.value" />.

   For example, GestureRight <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.condition.equals" /> 7 is true while the right hand shows a thumbs up.

5. Add the effects, as in the first part.

</Steps>

The list groups the parameters under <Label k="ad.ui.pick.parameter.avatar" /> (the avatar's own and its components'), <Label k="ad.ui.pick.parameter.doctor" />, <Label k="ad.ui.pick.parameter.vrchat" /> (VRChat's own, such as GestureLeft or Seated) and <Label k="ad.ui.chip.vrcfury" /> when the avatar has VRCFury toggles with a global parameter. You can also type a name into <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.parameter" /> yourself.

### Combine conditions

<Steps>

1. Under the <Label k="ad.ui.fires" /> line, press <Label k="ad.ui.when.add_and" /> to add a condition that must also be true.

   You should now see a group: a box that holds the first condition and a new row. The new row starts as a <Label k="ad.ui.when.kind.toggle" /> condition. Change its kind in the list at the top of the row.

2. To add a second way the trigger can fire, press <Label k="ad.ui.when.add_or" />.

   You should now see a second group, with <Label k="ad.ui.when.any_of" /> between the groups.

</Steps>

Inside a group, every condition must be true (<Label k="ad.ui.when.all_of" />). The trigger fires while any group is fully true. The <Label k="ad.ui.fires" /> line always says the whole rule in words. Use at most 4 groups of 4 conditions each: <Label k="ad.ui.when.add_and" /> switches off once a group holds 4.

To make the trigger work only while a menu switch is on, open the panel's <Label k="ad.ui.advanced_fold" /> fold. In <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.gate" />, pick a toggle and <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.toggle.on" /> or <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.toggle.off" />, or press <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.gate.add" /> to make a new toggle for it.

## Check it worked

- In the trigger's panel, press <Label k="ad.ui.preview.on" /> in the <Label k="ad.ui.preview" /> row. The Scene view shows the effects. Press <Label k="ad.ui.preview.default" /> to stop the preview.
- In Play mode with Gesture Manager, turn the followed toggle on, or make the gesture, and watch the effects come on. See [Your first toggle](../../start-here/first-toggle/#test-it) for Play mode.

## If it does not work

- A warning says the trigger refers to a toggle, parameter or blend shape that no longer exists: pick a new one under <Label k="ad.ui.trigger.when" />. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-trigger-source-missing).
- A warning says nothing on the avatar declares the parameter: check the spelling, or pick the name from the list. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-warn-parameter-type-unknown).
- The effect fights with a toggle that changes the same thing: the one AvatarDoc builds later wins, and triggers come after every menu control. Give the trigger and the toggle different things to change. See [the message](../../reference/messages/#ad-info-target-shared).
